# Concept Taxonomy

Every concept in the learning graph belongs to one of the 15 categories below.
They run roughly in teaching order, from mathematical and physical foundations,
through the hardware of the microscope, to computation, machine learning, and
the advanced techniques covered in the term project. The TaxonomyID is the
short code stored in `learning-graph.csv`; the category name is what the graph
viewer shows in its legend.

| TaxonomyID | Category | What belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| MATH | Mathematical Foundations | Complex exponentials and phasors, linear shift-invariant systems, convolution, Fourier transforms, special functions, linear algebra, probability, optimization, and Python. |
| WAVE | Wave Optics and Light | Maxwell's equations, plane and spherical waves, refractive index, polarization, interference and coherence, reflection and refraction, absorption, scattering, photons, Gaussian beams, and lasers. |
| GEOM | Geometric Optics | Ray and paraxial optics, thin and thick lenses, ray-transfer matrices, stops, pupils, chief and marginal rays, conjugate planes, 4f relays, and the Seidel aberrations. |
| DIFF | Diffraction and Image Formation | Numerical aperture, scalar diffraction, the angular spectrum, the pupil function, PSF and OTF, resolution criteria, axial resolution, and PSF engineering. |
| INST | Microscope Instrumentation | Infinity-corrected microscopes, objectives and immersion, tube lenses, light sources, Köhler illumination, filters and dichroics, relay optics, stages, and alignment. |
| FLUO | Fluorescence Fundamentals | Absorption and emission, the Jablonski diagram, lifetime and quantum yield, photobleaching and phototoxicity, epi-fluorescence and filter cubes, TIRF, and the photon budget. |
| DET | Photodetection and Sensors | Photon and detector noise, SNR models, CCD, EMCCD, sCMOS, PMT, APD and hybrid detectors, photon counting, and camera calibration. |
| CONT | Contrast Mechanisms | Amplitude and phase objects, brightfield, darkfield, Zernike phase contrast, polarization, DIC, interference and quantitative phase imaging, and fluorescence contrast. |
| SAMP | Digital Sampling and Data | Nyquist–Shannon sampling, pixel-size matching, aliasing, binning, temporal and axial sampling, bit depth, data rates, file formats, and acquisition control. |
| SCAN | Scanning and Nonlinear Systems | Laser-scanning architectures, galvanometric, resonant and acousto-optic scanners, confocal sectioning, ultrafast lasers, multiphoton excitation, harmonic generation, and FLIM. |
| AO | Adaptive Optics and Wavefronts | Wavefront aberrations and Zernike modes, Strehl ratio, sample-induced aberrations, deformable mirrors and SLMs, wavefront sensing, sensorless AO, and wavefront shaping. |
| REST | Computational Restoration | Forward models and noise models, inverse problems, deconvolution (inverse, Wiener, Richardson–Lucy), regularization, denoising, and restoration quality metrics. |
| QUANT | Quantitative Image Analysis | Preprocessing, registration, segmentation, feature extraction, localization and particle tracking, colocalization, spatial statistics, and analysis pipelines. |
| ML | Data-Driven and Smart Microscopy | Machine-learning basics, CNNs and U-Nets, learned denoising, restoration and segmentation, validation and hallucination, and real-time automated microscope control. |
| ADV | Advanced Techniques and Practice | Light-sheet and super-resolution methods, other emerging techniques, electron microscopy and CLEM, technique selection, system design, and reading, writing, and presenting research. |
