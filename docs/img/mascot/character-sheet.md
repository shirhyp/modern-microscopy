# Character Sheet: Lux the Tardigrade

The canonical identity document for Lux, the pedagogical
mascot for the **Advanced Topics in Modern Microscopy Techniques** textbook.
Every pose prompt and every piece of AI-generated content involving this
character must re-anchor to the description below — it is the source of truth
for visual and voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Lux
- **Species:** Tardigrade
- **Subject:** Modern optical microscopy
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's take a closer look!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Warm cream-tan — hex `#E8C9A0`, with a slightly darker tan belly and soft segment lines (`#C9A27A`)
- **Accent color:** Indigo — hex `#3F51B5` (the book's primary color), with small orange highlights — hex `#FF9800` (the book's accent color)
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round safety goggles with indigo frames and a light-blue lens tint, pushed up on top of the head (never covering the eyes); a short orange scarf around the neck
- **Body shape:** A chubby, rounded, barrel-shaped water bear with four segments and eight short, stubby legs, each tipped with tiny pale claws. Lux stands upright on the back two pairs of legs; the front pair acts as "arms" for gestures.
- **Expression:** Large, round, shiny dark eyes with a white highlight, rosy cheeks, and a small friendly smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact with a big head-to-body ratio, readable at icon size (about 90 px tall)
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector cartoon, clean dark outlines, soft cel shading, no gradients or textures

## Personality

- Curious — wants to see how everything works, down to the last photon
- Patient — happy to go through a derivation or a light path one step at a time
- Resilient — famously tough, like real tardigrades, and treats hard problems as a challenge rather than a threat
- Precise — cares about units, numbers, and doing the measurement right

## Voice

- Speaks in short, clear sentences and explains the "why" behind each idea
- Uses gentle optics and microscopy wordplay sparingly (focus, sharpen, resolve, zoom in)
- Treats the reader as a fellow instrument builder and investigator
- Signature phrases: "Let's take a closer look!", "Let's bring this into focus.", "Every photon counts."

## Pronouns

Lux has no gender. Always write "Lux" or *they/them*.

## Why This Mascot

The tardigrade is the microscopic animal almost every microscopist has looked
at, so it belongs to the world this book is about. Real tardigrades are famously
hard to destroy, which suits a demanding engineering course that asks students
to keep going through hard material. The name Lux is the unit of illuminance and
the Latin word for light, it is gender-neutral, and the goggles and indigo and
orange accents tie the character to the lab and to the book's color theme.
